The shift

When software acts, authority becomes the question.

For years, AI primarily suggested while people executed. Increasingly, AI agents can initiate transactions, access systems and take actions on behalf of people and organizations.

Whether an action is useful is not enough. It must also fall within the authority actually granted.

Wakāla · وكالة

An old concept. A new frontier.

In Arabic legal and commercial traditions, wakāla concerns acting on behalf of another. Its vocabulary offers a powerful conceptual lens for thinking about identity, scope and accountability in autonomous AI — without treating legal agency and software authorization as identical.

الموكِّلthe principal

The person or entity granting authority.

The human or organization on whose behalf an AI agent acts.

الوكيلAlwakile · the agent

A representative entrusted to act for another.

The identified agent carrying out a permitted operation.

الموكَّل فيهthe assigned matter

The subject and boundaries of the delegated task.

Scoped permissions: actions, resources and transaction limits.

الصيغةthe form of authorization

The expression of an agency arrangement.

A documented grant and evidence of approval.

العزلremoval of the agent

The ending of an agency appointment, subject to applicable rules.

Revocation mechanisms and propagation of changed permissions.

These are illustrative parallels, not legal equivalences or claims of universally immediate revocation. The requirements and effects of agency depend on the jurisdiction and context.
